Paddling Areas

May 1–3, 2026 · Reference one-pagers for planning group distribution
What this is

Alex — I've put together a first draft of some paddling area one-pagers to try and help thing through accomodating 10 groups on the water for three days. Each paddling area includes a launch (or pair of launches), what's available from the launch(es), and the conditions (tides and currents) for May 1–3. These are not polished, just put these together to help think through how things could work on the water for the groups; if you think something like this could be helpful for you or the coaches/participants, these could be a starting point for something folks could reference. I'm not attached to you or anyone using these, just wanted to share in case they spark joy.

Each one-pager is a standalone printable page — launch logistics, tide and current predictions, a marine chart with numbered play and lunch spots, and tables of what's at each spot.

Planning note
Minimum total capacity is 15 groups across the five areas — 50% slack for 10 groups. Ala Spit is the tightest, capped at one group; everything else has room. If you're thinking in terms of "one area per group level" or "one area per focus," you've got plenty of distribution patterns to choose from.

Bowman Bay

Fidalgo Island · 1 launch · 3+ groups

Deception Pass State Park on the Fidalgo side. Seven play spots (Bowman Bay, Deception Island, Lighthouse Point, Northwest Island, Reservation Head, Rosario Head, Urchin Rocks) and seven lunch spots. The most play-rich area.

Deception Pass

Whidbey Island · 1 launch · 3 groups

Cornet Bay launch inside the state park. Direct access to the Narrows — the signature 4.7+ kt midday flood — plus Boat Pass, Canoe Pass, and Strawberry Island. Three conditions tables (tide + 2 currents).

Open one-pager →

Ala Spit

NE Whidbey · 1 launch · 1 group

Small county park at the NE corner of Whidbey. Protected launch on the west side of the spit. Access to Hope Island, Skagit Island, Kiket Island, and Hoypus Point. Skagit Bay channel is ebb-dominant at midday.

Open one-pager →

Burrows-Allan

Fidalgo Head · 2 launches · 5+ groups

Washington Park at the tip of Fidalgo Head (3+ groups) and Cabana Lane Water Access (2 groups) — the only two-launch area. Burrows Pass and Allan Pass run 2.6–2.7 kt midday flood, gentler than Deception Pass. Burrows Island Lighthouse lunch.

Open one-pager →

Admiralty Inlet

Whidbey Island · 1 launch · 3+ groups

Keystone Boat Launch at Fort Casey State Park. Big water, significant ferry traffic, most exposed of the five. Admiralty Inlet currents run 2.5–2.9 kt midday flood. Thinner place menu than the others — intentionally.
